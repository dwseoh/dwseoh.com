I've had a personal site for years, but it was always a *destination* — a place
you land, read a bio, and leave. This is the part I kept meaning to add: a room
where I can actually **think out loud**.

So, a blog. Or a newsletter. Or a notebook that happens to be public. I haven't
decided what to call it, and I don't think it matters.

## What I want this to be

A few loose rules I'm giving myself:

- **Short is fine.** A single good paragraph beats a draft I never ship.
- **Specific over general.** One real detail from a real week, not advice.
- **Written like I talk.** If I wouldn't say it to a friend, I'll cut it.

> The scariest moment is always just before you start.
> — Stephen King

## What I'll probably write about

Software, mostly — I'm studying [Software Engineering at
Waterloo](https://uwaterloo.ca) and spend my co-ops shipping things I'm still a
little surprised they let me touch. But also music, because I conduct and
compose when I'm not at a keyboard, and the two rhyme more than you'd expect.
